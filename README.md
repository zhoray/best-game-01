Orbwarden is an original gravity-well arcade game for the desktop browser. You steer a drifting warden orb with the mouse or arrow keys while your gravity well bends the fall of falling motes. Bend motes of your well's current colour into the well to score and build your combo; let the other colours fall past. Gold motes score from any colour, black void shards ignore gravity and damage your shield on contact, and the well's colour keeps cycling faster as the clock runs down. Reach 1200 points before the 150 seconds run out, or survive to the best score you can when your shield of 3 is gone.

To play: open game/index.html in a desktop browser. No build step and no network access are needed.

Layout: main.ts builds the hand-in (copies the game and the screenshots and writes the dossier). game/ holds the playable static game. screenshots/ holds renders of the game itself. shots.json lists the screenshot scenes.
